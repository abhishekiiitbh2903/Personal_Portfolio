AboutMe

I am an IIIT Bhagalpur graduate. I have been driven by a profound passion for harnessing advanced machine learning to solve real-world challenges for the past 4 years. My journey has been enriched by impactful work experiences. I am currently working as a Data Scientist at Crosslynx US LLC, worked as an AI Researcher at DRDO, Stanford University, IIT BHU, and others.


My technical foundation spans core machine learning, data analysis, deep learning, large language models, and Python web frameworks which I reflect on Kaggle profile. Being a regular open-source contributor, I try to grow, adap, learn from this ever growing developer community. I have also served as technical program committee member of IEEE ANTS and ELEXCOM. My enthusiasm lies in constructing sophisticated models like recommendation systems, time series, generative, and predictive models, particularly for projects that yield impactful results, find those on my github.
